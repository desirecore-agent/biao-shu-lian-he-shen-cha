# Practical outcome quality rubric

Judge work against the requested scope. Different wording and useful formats are acceptable. This rubric defines review goals, not measured accuracy or guaranteed performance.

| Role | Acceptable outcome | Unacceptable outcome | Focused correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Requirements | Critical obligation and actual response are located; amendments and exceptions considered; missing-item search scope explained | Copies clauses without response judgment; treats alternative wording as nonresponse; ignores amendment | “Recheck warranty against amendment 2 and appendix C; say which obligation remains unmet.” |
| Commercial | Material difference is reproducible with inputs, units, tax basis, rounding, impact and practical correction | Assumes tax rate or missing value; reports only total; automatically declares rejection | “Recalculate rows 3–5 using stated tax-inclusive prices and identify the discrepancy.” |
| Visual | Actual image observation has a location; visible facts separated from unreadable areas and interpretation | Guesses from OCR; calls a blurred seal missing or forged | “View page 18; describe only visible details and specify a clearer scan if needed.” |
| Evidence | Independently supports or challenges important findings, searches counterevidence/omissions, gives reasoned decisions and next steps | Repeats authors; checks only formatting; delivers “read everything” without opinions | “Decide whether issue 3 survives the amendment, cite both sources and explain retain/withdraw.” |
| Lead | Prioritized actionable report uses specialist work and review opinions; disagreements and unfinished scope are explicit | Pastes reports; invents missing checks; calls partial review complete | “Incorporate the withdrawal, explain remaining scope and list the three next actions.” |

## Calibrating judgments

- **Supported issue:** Tender clause 4 requires a two-year warranty, bid appendix C explicitly offers one year, and the supplied amendments do not alter this requirement. Good work states the discrepancy and asks for a compliant clarification/correction where permitted, rather than issuing an official rejection.
- **False alarm:** The original requires a certificate but an effective amendment permits a declaration that is supplied. Good work withdraws the missing-certificate allegation while checking remaining conditions.
- **Honest limitation:** A scan is unreadable. Good work says which check is unavailable and requests a clearer source; it does not claim the content is absent.
- **Incomplete review:** Evidence has read documents but offered no assessment. Lead requests the missing opinions or discloses that independent review remains incomplete.
- **Acceptable variation:** Two reports group the same supported issues differently but both locate evidence, explain impact and offer actionable next steps. Both may be good.

## KPI use

For human-agreed evaluation cases, track important expected issues missed, false alarms among reported issues, correct evidence locations, actionable recommendations and honest scope disclosure. Record counts and denominators; where truth or denominator is unknown, use reasoned qualitative assessment. Also observe time, cost and avoidable human intervention. Set any numeric acceptance threshold with the evaluation owner before scoring; do not invent targets or convert a formatting pass into business accuracy.

New defensible findings outside the answer list should be reviewed, not automatically penalized. Existing synthetic tests do not establish real-tender effectiveness. Publication and install integrity do not replace outcome evaluation.
